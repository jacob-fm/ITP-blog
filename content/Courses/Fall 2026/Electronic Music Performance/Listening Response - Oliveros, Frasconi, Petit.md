I listened to [[Pauline Oliveros]]’ [Applebox](https://electronicmusicperformance.wordpress.com/2026/02/10/pauline-oliveros-applebox/), a few sections of [[Miguel Frasconi]]'s [Modulisme Session 040](https://modular-station.com/modulisme/session/40/), and [[Phillipe Petit]]'s [Стоп!](https://emerge.bandcamp.com/track/non-la-guerre).

I mostly thought about which songs/sounds were pleasant or unpleasant, and whether the unpleasant ones were intentionally so. I started with the Pauline Oliveros piece, which was very pleasant. It's like a proto-ASMR video. Frasconi was a mixed bag. There were moments where I started to enjoy listening, but they were often interrupted by harsh, grating sounds. For example, in the 3rd song, *Bugs+Glass 4May20*, I found the small droplet sounds satisfying, but the scraping sounds literally made me wince at times. I wonder if other people find it as unpleasant as I do, but still find something compelling in the harshness of it, or whether they actually enjoy those parts. Petit's piece, it seems to me, is meant to be unsettling, but I did not find it unpleasant. I actually enjoyed listening to it more on a second listen. There's some really nice texture going on with the sounds he chooses.
