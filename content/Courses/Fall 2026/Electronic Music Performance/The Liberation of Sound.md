I had trouble understanding the first page or so of this text, at least in any intellectual sense. Varese describes music of the future in abstract terms beyond melody. He talks about colliding "sound-masses", "dimensions" of music, and "zones of intensities". There were parts of this discussion that reminded me of feelings I've had at times when listening to music, though I can't say for sure if the moments I'm thinking of are what Varese is describing here.
