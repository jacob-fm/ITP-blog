## Performers and Instruments

The piece will involve **three performers**:

- **Performer 1:** Uses TouchDesigner and MediaPipe to manipulate a drum sample through gestures, controlling its speed, pitch, and playback direction. Their interface will be projected onto a screen for the audience.
- **Performer 2:** Uses a MIDI controller to trigger non-musical vocal samples, including excerpts from poems and speeches. They will also control the wet/dry mix of a vocoder.
- **Performer 3:** Uses a MIDI controller to trigger musical loops, including chords and melodies.

## Structure

The piece will combine predetermined samples and loops with live manipulation and improvisation. Each performer will control their own set of sounds, layering and interacting with the other performers to create a cohesive composition. The exact structure is still being developed.

## Visuals

Performer 1's TouchDesigner interface will be projected during the performance, allowing the audience to see the gesture-controlled sample manipulation in real time.

## Style and Inspiration

The project draws inspiration from Max Mathews's Radio Baton, particularly its use of physical gestures to control electronic sound. I plan to explore a similar approach using TouchDesigner and MediaPipe.

The vocal samples are also inspired by Pierre Schaeffer's musique concrète and its use of recorded, non-musical sounds as compositional material. Rather than environmental recordings, I am particularly interested in exploring excerpts of speech, poetry, and the human voice.
