**Instructor**: [Mason Mann](https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/people/mason-mann)

**Course Description**:

This course is designed for instrumentalists and electronic musicians who want to explore performing with electronic music (analog or digital/software-based) using historical and contemporary methods in an ensemble performance environment-- incorporating techniques for live sound processing, synthesis, or interactive elements into their work. We will work from repertoire also investigating very up-to-the-minute and recent works/compositions. The focus is on individual and ensemble work, improvisation, live electronics, live sound processing etc.

The course aims to improve each student’s understanding of the aesthetic implications of the use of electronic music techniques, alternative controllers and devices, in small and large ensemble situations, working toward a planned performance at the end of the semester, as well as other performance opportunities as they arise. In addition to in-class work, there will be required readings and music listening with written responses via Brightspace, as well as required concert attendance/reviews of 2 concerts/events. Students are responsible for the preparation of any electronic instruments or software they choose to use the weekly classes and should be prepared to play in class every week
